Support

We aim to reply within a few days

Email olaldetomas1@gmail.com and tell us what happened. It helps a lot if you include your app version — you can find it at the bottom of the Profile tab — and the link or photo you were trying to import.

Common questions

A video wouldn't import

We can only read videos that are publicly viewable. Private accounts, deleted posts, age-restricted videos and members-only content will fail. Very long videos are also rejected, because reading them is slow and expensive. If a link is public and still fails, send it to us.

The recipe came out wrong

The AI misreads things sometimes, particularly handwriting, quantities and unit conversions. Every field is editable — tap the recipe and correct it. Please always check the original source before cooking, especially if anyone eating has an allergy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Account Actions → Delete account. That permanently removes your profile, all of your saved recipes and your sign-in account. It cannot be undone. If it fails, email us and we will do it for you.

Where is my data stored, and who can see it?

Your recipes are private to your account. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what we store, which providers process it, and how long we keep it.

Do you have my recipes if I reinstall?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google or Apple account and your recipes will be there, as long as you have not deleted your account.

Reporting a security issue

If you find a vulnerability, please email olaldetomas1@gmail.com with the details rather than exploiting it. We will get back to you.
