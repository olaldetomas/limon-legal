Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

The short version

limon stores your name, email address and the recipes you save. To turn a video, photo or voice note into a recipe, that content is sent to Google's Gemini AI service for processing and is not kept afterwards.

There is no advertising, no analytics tracking and nothing is sold to anyone. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

limon is a recipe app operated by Tomas Olalde ("we", "us"), based in [[ FILL: country ]]. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

For any privacy question or request, email olaldetomas1@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account information

You sign in with Google or Apple. We receive your name, your email address and a unique account identifier from that provider. We never see or store your Google or Apple password. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only ever receive the anonymous relay address, which works exactly the same for us.

Recipes you save

The content of your saved recipes: title, description, ingredients, quantities, servings, cooking steps, a link back to the original source where there is one, and which ingredients or steps you have ticked off.

Content you submit for processing

Depending on the features you use, this can include photos of recipes you scan, short voice recordings when you use voice input, the messages you type to the in-app cooking assistant, and links to videos or posts you share into the app.

Technical and security logs

Our server records the account identifier making a request, the endpoint used, the link you asked us to import, timestamps, response status, processing time, and error details when something fails. Our hosting provider additionally logs standard request metadata such as IP address. We use these records to diagnose failures, prevent abuse and control processing costs.

What we do not collect

limon contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracker. We do not collect advertising identifiers, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not collect your location, contacts or health data. The app has no purchases, so we never receive payment or card details.

3. Why we use it

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To turn the videos, photos, voice notes and links you give us into structured recipes, and to answer your questions about them.
  • To store and sync your recipes so they are there next time.
  • To keep the service secure, to enforce usage limits, and to detect and prevent abuse.
  • To fix bugs and to answer you when you contact support.

Under the GDPR, we rely on performance of a contract for everything needed to actually provide the app, on our legitimate interests for security, abuse prevention and cost control, and on your consent for device permissions such as camera and microphone, which you can withdraw in your device settings at any time.

We may also disclose data if we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to protect our rights or someone's safety.

5. How the AI processing works

This is the part worth understanding properly, because it is where your content leaves your device.

Photos, voice recordings, chat messages and video links are sent to Google's Gemini API through our own server, which holds the API key. Google processes them and returns text. Under the paid Gemini API terms we use, Google does not use this content to train its models. Google may retain it briefly for abuse monitoring under its own terms.

We do not keep the media itself. Voice recordings and scanned photos are processed in transit and never written to our database. When a video has to be downloaded to be read, the file is deleted from our server immediately after processing. What persists is only the resulting recipe text, saved to your account because that is the point of the app.

Automated extraction is not perfect. Please see the accuracy warning in our Terms of Use before relying on a recipe, particularly for allergies.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your profile and recipes — until you delete them, or until you delete your account.
  • Technical and security logs — about 30 days, then automatically discarded.
  • Sign-in account data — held by Clerk until you delete your account.

7. Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Profile → Account Actions → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, every recipe you have saved, and your sign-in account. It cannot be undone, and we do not keep a backup copy for you afterwards.

If that fails for any reason, email olaldetomas1@gmail.com and we will do it for you.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to receive a copy in a portable format, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent you have given. To exercise any of these, email olaldetomas1@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA or the UK and you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

9. International transfers

Our service providers are largely based in the United States, so your data is transferred outside the EEA and the UK. Those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another valid safeguard in each provider's data processing agreement.

10. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. Database access is restricted per user at the row level, so your recipes are only readable with a valid session token issued to your own account. Your session token is held in your device's secure storage. Our AI provider key is held only on our server and is never present in the app.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

limon is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app or by email.
